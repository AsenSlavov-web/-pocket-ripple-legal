Open an account-deletion email

Subject: No Tile Left — account deletion request. The publisher, Daniel Nikolaev Chukov, handles incoming requests. This website provides email requests rather than an automatic deletion form. Sending an email starts a deletion request and does not instantly delete the game account.

What to include

Give the game tag or sign-in email, if known, and say that you want the account and associated data deleted. If it concerns a child, say that you are the parent or guardian. Include only information needed to locate the relevant account. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens, deletion recovery tickets, bank details or unnecessary identity documents.

Ownership and parent requests

A public nickname or tag alone is not proof of ownership. The publisher will provide an appropriate verified-email or authenticated ownership check. Where necessary, parent/guardian authority is checked proportionately. If you no longer have access to the original sign-in method, explain that; do not create another account or reinstall just to submit the request. The publisher will discuss reasonable alternative verification and explain if the record cannot safely be identified.

What deletion affects

Covered cloud deletion includes the No Tile Left authentication record (Firebase identity), game profile, connected progress and puzzle attempts, ranking, owned cosmetics/equipment, Paint Buckets and reward/purchase records, and associated first-party events. Earned items and cloud progress are lost with that account. It is deletion, not merely deactivation or removal from a leaderboard. It does not delete your Google account or Gmail mailbox.

Unrelated local guest saves, independent offline devices and files you exported remain separate. To remove a local save, clear the game's app/browser storage on that device; export first only if you want to keep your own copy. The publisher cannot remotely erase your exports or unrelated guest data. Deletion removes covered active game records. Provider-controlled backups and security logs may expire on a different schedule. Firebase states that customer-deleted Authentication information is removed from its live and backup systems within 180 days. Our publisher does not operate a separate game backup archive. Before restored data is made available, completed deletions must be reapplied from the deletion register; restored account services stay disabled until that check is complete.

Timing, failures and limited retention

The publisher handles requests without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines. Where the GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month of receipt. A necessary extension of up to two further months is explained within the first month, with reasons. We may request proportionate additional information where identity is reasonably in doubt; we do not require passwords or identity tokens.

A provider or database failure keeps removal pending for follow-up and retry; it is not reported as completed. Completion means covered active records have been removed. Minimal completed recovery/security records remain for 90 days; pending work remains until it finishes. A minimized pseudonymous source identifier prevents the same legacy save from receiving opening credit repeatedly. It is kept only while that import mechanism remains supported, with necessity reviewed at least every six months. When the mechanism is permanently retired, imports are blocked first and the residual source receipts are deleted. A verified child-data case is reviewed promptly for deletion of any unnecessary identifier. These receipts are not anonymous. Closed privacy and support correspondence is normally kept for no more than six months after closure. A specific legal obligation or unresolved dispute may justify retaining only the relevant records longer; the reason and review date are recorded.
