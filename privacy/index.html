Local play and device storage

Local play keeps puzzle attempts and results, hearts and refill times, Paint Buckets, reward receipts, collection progress, owned avatars/equipment, selected cosmetics and preferences on your device. These records support saving, restoring and avoiding duplicate rewards. Core local gameplay does not require creating a game account. Removing app/browser storage can remove that device's save. Files you choose to export and independent offline copies remain under your control.

Our legal pages have no advertising SDK, analytics tags, sign-in form or game database connection. Hosting providers still handle technical requests such as IP address, browser information and security/delivery logs. The private web preview also uses its host's access service, under that provider's terms. Local play does not mean that online hosting or a configured SDK transmits no information.

Artwork and AI tools

Some pre-generated artwork and development materials were prepared with AI tools. The current game does not send player inputs to an AI model or offer an in-game generative-AI feature. Hosting the private web preview on OpenAI Sites is separate from using an AI model in the game.

Connected data, when the service is available

Connected features are currently unavailable. The implemented account system uses Google/Firebase Authentication for identity and the game's existing hosted database for progress and earned items. When activated for eligible users, Firebase can process an email address, provider profile information such as name or profile-photo URL, provider identifiers, a Firebase user ID, credentials and security/request information such as IP address, SDK/app metadata and user agent. The game requests identity sign-in, not access to Gmail, contacts or Drive. Authentication credentials are not public game-profile fields.

The game account can store submitted puzzle attempts/swipe traces, progress and stars, hearts, Daily/Mastery results, inventory, earned currency and reward/purchase receipts, and recovery/import records. These provide requested features and protect saves and rewards. Paint Buckets are earned virtual currency; the current game has no card payment, real-money purchase or billing integration.

A public game profile/ranking can show the chosen nickname, game avatar, public game tag, equipped cosmetics, verified progress and rank. Avoid putting private information in a nickname. Email, identity-provider identifiers and full saves are not included in the public profile response. Public profiles/ranking for children are not activated.

Children, parents and teenagers

The intended family version is for a mixed audience of children approximately six and above, teenagers and adults. This is a product audience description, not an age rating or a statement that every release requirement has passed. The Android family version is not yet publicly released.

Connected child accounts, optional child analytics, public child profiles/ranking and child advertising are not activated. We do not treat a “parent approved” checkbox as verified parental consent. Android preparation includes a neutral, device-local age step before optional online SDK use; unknown age receives conservative treatment. Advertising remains disabled in this preparation until the exact integration and required checks are verified. These source safeguards have not yet been certified on a final installed Android release.

Parents or guardians can contact the publisher to ask about a child's information, request access or correction where applicable, withdraw an applicable permission, or request deletion. We will use proportionate checks for authority and account ownership. Do not send a child's password, token or recovery ticket. If we learn that unnecessary child information was collected, we will investigate and remove it or otherwise handle it as required by applicable law. We will not activate connected services for children without the required notices, lawful basis and verified parental-consent process where needed.

Advertising and optional measurement

The private web game has no active web-ad publisher. Android source preparation contains Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen and Google's consent platform (UMP), but advertising is disabled pending release verification. Earlier test builds may have used restricted test ads; test or non-personalized treatment does not prove zero collection.

If advertising is later activated in an eligible release, Google documents processing of IP/general location, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The exact behavior depends on the SDK, device controls, age treatment, region and consent. Children and unknown-age users must not receive advertising from an unverified integration, personalized advertising or advertising-identifier transmission. Any future teen/adult advertising requires appropriate age treatment and regional consent. Available advertising privacy choices belong in Settings when the configured consent platform requires them.

Optional first-party gameplay measurement is separate from essential save, security and reward records, and is not currently enabled in production. The game does not declare Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics in its current dependencies. That does not mean other SDKs or hosting providers never process diagnostics.

Retention and backups

  • Connected profile, progress, inventory and earned-currency records are kept while the account exists and removed through the verified deletion process. Cloud sign-in is not currently activated. Local saves remain on the device until cleared by the player.
  • The application does not persist routine HTTP request logs or log passwords and tokens. Any application-controlled routine technical logging must be minimized and expire within 30 days. Optional first-party event rows are subject to a 30-day cleanup routine; infrastructure providers control their own service logs separately.
  • Closed privacy and support correspondence is normally kept for no more than six months after closure. A specific legal obligation or unresolved dispute may justify retaining only the relevant records longer; the reason and review date are recorded.
  • Completed deletion recovery and narrowly necessary security records are kept for 90 days after completion. Recovery tickets are random and stored hashed on the server. Expired completed records are removed during maintenance. A failed deletion remains pending until actually completed and receives operational follow-up.
  • A minimized pseudonymous source identifier prevents the same legacy save from receiving opening credit repeatedly. It is kept only while that import mechanism remains supported, with necessity reviewed at least every six months. When the mechanism is permanently retired, imports are blocked first and the residual source receipts are deleted. A verified child-data case is reviewed promptly for deletion of any unnecessary identifier. These receipts are not anonymous.

Deletion removes covered active game records. Provider-controlled backups and security logs may expire on a different schedule. Firebase states that customer-deleted Authentication information is removed from its live and backup systems within 180 days. Our publisher does not operate a separate game backup archive. Before restored data is made available, completed deletions must be reapplied from the deletion register; restored account services stay disabled until that check is complete.

Deletion and your rights

Request deletion of your No Tile Left account and associated data without reinstalling the game or signing in to ChatGPT. Covered cloud deletion removes the account identity and associated profile, cloud progress, attempts, inventory, Paint Buckets, reward records and ranking, subject only to the limited retention explained above. Clearing a local guest save is separate and does not delete cloud records. A cloud deletion cannot remotely erase unrelated guest saves, exports or independent offline copies.

The publisher handles requests without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines. Where the GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month of receipt. A necessary extension of up to two further months is explained within the first month, with reasons. We may request proportionate additional information where identity is reasonably in doubt; we do not require passwords or identity tokens.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing; you can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Requests are normally free. Any lawful restriction or refusal will be explained with the available complaint/judicial options. You can complain to a competent supervisory authority, including Bulgaria's Commission for Personal Data Protection. Regional rights and children's consent ages differ; intended international distribution is not a claim of universal compliance or availability.

Security and changes

Online requests use HTTPS. Account-specific actions verify identity and authorization; automatic deletion requires recent authentication when it is available. Failed deletion work is kept retryable and stale account writes are blocked. We limit access to support information and ask only for what is needed. No system is perfectly secure. Local files are not end-to-end encrypted cloud storage, and you should keep exports private.

We will update the effective date when this policy changes and give additional notice or seek consent when required. Contact details are maintained together for both legal pages and may change with the publisher's correspondence address.
